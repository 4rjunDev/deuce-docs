# Support

Need help with Tennis IQ, or want to report a problem?

- Email: **[tennisiqnews@gmail.com](mailto:tennisiqnews@gmail.com)**
- Please include your device model, iOS version, and the app version shown in Settings.

## Deleting your account

Open **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. Your profile, trivia answers, streaks, and badges are removed immediately and cannot be recovered. If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
