# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Deuce ("the app", "we", "us") is a daily tennis trivia app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in plain text.

**Profile information.** You may choose to add a username and a short bio. Your username, bio, streaks, badges, and leaderboard rank are visible to other users of the app.

**Trivia activity.** To run the game we store your daily answers, whether they were correct, your current and longest streaks, and your monthly leaderboard results and badges.

**Safety actions.** If you block or report another player, we store who you blocked or reported and the reason you gave, so we can hide a blocked player’s bio from you and review reports.

**Diagnostics.** The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to operate the app: to sign you in, show your profile, score your daily answers, and run the monthly leaderboard and badges.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with **Supabase**, which hosts our database and authentication. We may also disclose information if required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Settings → Delete account**, which permanently removes your profile and trivia history. Backups are purged on a rolling basis after deletion.

## Your choices

- You can edit your username and bio in the app.
- You can block or report another player from their profile. Blocking hides their bio from you.
- You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

## Children

Deuce is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
